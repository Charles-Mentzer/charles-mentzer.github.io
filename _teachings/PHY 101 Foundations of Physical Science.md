Course Objectives:  To successfully complete this course, students will:
•	Demonstrate safe laboratory skills and data collection while employing the scientific method. 
•	Perform simple calculations and understand the result in the context of the problem posed. 
•	Describe the concepts of motion, forces, energy, waves, electricity, and magnetism using classical physics.
•	Describe the concepts of atomic structure, elements, and matter using modern physics and chemistry. 
•	Diagram our solar system and explain how it relates to the known universe.
•	Describe Earth’s internal structure and the dynamic nature of tectonic plates using concepts from earth science. 
•	Describe the Earth’s atmospheric processes that cause weather and climate using concepts from earth science.

## Course Overview
The course provides a study of basic concepts of physics, chemistry, astronomy, geology, and meteorology and their interrelation in the physical world. This course includes a laboratory component and is intended for non-science majors.
## Prerequisites
Prerequisite/Co-requisite:  Eligible placement score for MTH140, MTH142, MTH160 or higher or concurrent enrollment in MTH095 or any math corequisite.
