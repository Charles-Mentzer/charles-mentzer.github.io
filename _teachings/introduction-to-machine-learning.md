title: PHY162 General Astronomy
description: This is an introductory astronomy course with no laboratory component. It surveys solar system bodies, stellar evolution, galaxies, the universe, and the history of human discovery.
course_id: PHY 162
Course Content:
1.	Our Place in the Universe
2.	A Study of Early Astronomy
3.	Light, Matter and Energy
4.	The Solar System
5.	The Sun
6.	Star Evolution
7.	Neutron Stars and Black Holes

Course Objectives:
1.	Perform simple calculations and understand the result in the context of the problem posed.
2.	Describe the concepts of motion, forces, energy, and waves.
3.	Describe the concepts of elements and matter.
4.	Diagram our solar system and explain how it relates to the known universe. 
5.	Describe the basic motions of the solar system and the effects on the earth.
6.	Outline how the science of astronomy has developed over the ages.
7.	Describe the composition of the sun and the lifecycle of stars.
8.	Describe the structure and makeup of our Universe and composition of galaxies.
