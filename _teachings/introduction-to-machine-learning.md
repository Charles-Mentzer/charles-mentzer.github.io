title: PHY162 General Astronomy
description: This is an introductory astronomy course with no laboratory component. It surveys solar system bodies, stellar evolution, galaxies, the universe, and the history of human discovery.
course_id: PHY 162
Course Content:
• Our Place in the Universe
• A Study of Early Astronomy
• Light, Matter and Energy
• The Solar System
• The Sun
• Star Evolution
• Neutron Stars and Black Holes

Course Objectives:
• Perform simple calculations and understand the result in the context of the problem posed.
• Describe the concepts of motion, forces, energy, and waves.
• Describe the concepts of elements and matter.
• Diagram our solar system and explain how it relates to the known universe. 
• Describe the basic motions of the solar system and the effects on the earth.
• Outline how the science of astronomy has developed over the ages.
• Describe the composition of the sun and the lifecycle of stars.
• Describe the structure and makeup of our Universe and composition of galaxies.
