## LAWS Observatory

LAWS Observatory, located atop the Physics & Astronomy building on the University of Missouri campus in Columbia, MO, houses a number of small telescopes, a computer room, and an astronomy exhibit room featuring a collection of science demonstrations. The observatory is open to the public every Wednesday night.

## 2023 Partial Solar Eclipse 

I helped organize a public partial solar eclipse viewing event on campus, hosted by the Department of Physics and Astronomy at Lowry Mall. Visitors were equipped to observe the eclipse safely, with telescopes set up on-site and eclipse glasses provided free of charge. Alongside fellow astronomy enthusiasts, I was on hand as a guide to answer questions and share more about the science behind the event, turning it into both an educational outreach opportunity and a memorable shared experience under the sky.
