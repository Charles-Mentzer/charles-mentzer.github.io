## Course Overview The course provides a study of basic concepts of physics, chemistry, astronomy, geology, and meteorology and their interrelation in the physical world. This course includes a laboratory component and is intended for non-science majors. ## Prerequisites Prerequisite/Co-requisite: Eligible placement score for MTH140, MTH142, MTH160 or higher or concurrent enrollment in MTH095 or any math corequisite. ## Textbooks Text: Title: An Introduction to Physical Science Author: James Shipman, Jerry D. Wilson, Charles A. Higgins, Bo Lou Edition: 15th Edition Publisher: Cengage
